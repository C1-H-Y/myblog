## Profile

My name is **Haoyu Chen**. I am currently an undergraduate student majoring in Physics at Tsinghua University. I am broadly interested in condensed matter theory and quantum physics, with a particular fascination for the emergent phenomena that arise when many quantum degrees of freedom interact with each other. I enjoy approaching physical problems from both analytical and computational perspectives, and I use this blog to document my learning notes, derivations, and research reflections.

## Research Interests

- **Non-equilibrium phenomena in many-body systems** — thermalization, many-body localization, and the dynamics of isolated quantum systems.
- **Quantum tunneling in transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDCs)** — especially light-driven tunneling, and their implications for nanoscale devices.
- **Strongly correlated physics** — emergent orders and phase transitions in interacting quantum matter.
